Sales Performance Analysis & Dashboard

An Excel-based data analysis project focused on exploring sales performance, identifying trends, and presenting business insights through an interactive dashboard.

Dataset Description

The project uses a synthetic sales dataset containing 100 records covering the years 2024 and 2025.

The dataset includes the following fields:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Region, State, and City
- Customer Segment
- Product Category and Subcategory
- Product
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Discount
- Sales
- Profit
- Year and Month

Tools & Methods

Tool: Microsoft Excel

**Methods Used:**
- Data Preparation and Organization
- Calculated Columns using Excel Formulas
- PivotTables for Data Aggregation
- Sales and Profit Analysis
- Year-over-Year Performance Comparison
- KPI Calculation
- Dashboard Design and Data Visualization

## Key Findings

- Total sales amounted to 191,112.35 across 100 orders.
- Technology was the largest sales category, but its sales declined significantly in 2025.
- Machines recorded the largest decline among Technology subcategories, with sales decreasing by approximately 91.8%.
- The West region experienced the largest regional sales decline, approximately 63.1%.
- Consumer segment sales decreased by approximately 71.3%, while Corporate and Home Office segments recorded growth.

## Dashboard Preview

The Excel dashboard provides a visual summary of sales performance through:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Sales by Year
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment

This project uses synthetic data created for learning and portfolio demonstration purposes. Profit was calculated using an assumed 40% margin on sales. The dataset does not represent actual business transactions.
